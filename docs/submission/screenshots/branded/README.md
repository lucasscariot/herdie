# App Store artwork

Created from the iPhone and iPad simulator captures in the parent folder, using the built-in imagegen tool. The three final PNG files are opaque and exported to Apple's supported portrait dimensions.

- iPhone terminal: 1284 × 2778. Headline: Your terminal. Anywhere. Supporting copy: Connect to your Herdr sessions over SSH.
- iPhone writing: 1284 × 2778. Headline: Write it. Send it. Supporting copy: A little more space for your next prompt.
- iPad writing: 2064 × 2752. Headline: Room for your next idea. Supporting copy: Write longer prompts. Keep your draft.

Prompt direction: a charcoal background with a restrained violet glow, white lowercase Herdie wordmark, large white headline, lavender supporting copy, and the supplied app screenshot as a large rounded card. Preserve the app controls and text. No additional hardware, status bar, device island, product claims, or badges.

The generated composition is marketing artwork, not a pixel-identical simulator capture. Original captures are retained separately for comparison. The first generated phone concept had a duplicated status bar and was discarded.
