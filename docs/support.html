Herdie support

Herdie connects your iPhone or iPad directly to a Mac or Linux host running Herdr over SSH.

Before connecting

  • Install Herdr on your host and confirm it runs there.
  • Enable SSH access and make sure the host is reachable from your device.
  • Have the hostname or IP address, SSH port, username, and authentication method ready.
  • Verify the host-key fingerprint against a trusted source before accepting it in Herdie.

Herdie does not provide a hosted computer, SSH account, or AI subscription.

Connection problems

Confirm that the hostname, port, and username are correct. If you use a private network or Tailscale, confirm that your device can reach the host through that network. In Herdie, open the connection details for the error message and use the connection-help screen.

A changed SSH host key can mean the host was rebuilt or its identity changed. Verify the new fingerprint with the host operator before trusting it.

Report an issue

Email lucas@scariot.fr for support. Include your device model, iOS version, Herdie version/build, and steps to reproduce the problem. Describe the authentication method without including the credential.

Remove passwords, private keys, tokens, private hostnames, and sensitive terminal content from screenshots and logs.
